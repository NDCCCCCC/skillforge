## SQL / 正文

```sql
select 我司渠道 from wuh_dw.fr_fgs_bd_list2


,b.manage_rate as 管理费用率总计_预算
,b.change_rate as 其他变动费用率总计_预算
,b.reception_rate as 前台业务绩效率小计_分渠道
,b.coordinate_rate as 统筹销售费用率小计_销管
,b.midback_rate as 中后台业务绩效率小计_预算
from wuh_dw.fr_fgs_bd_list2 a
left join wuh_dw.fr_fgs_gkac_finance b
on a.部门组名称 = b.jg
and a.渠道细分 = b.channel_sub 
and a.能源类型 = b.fuel_type
and a.商用私家识别 = b.usage_name_b


when '' then ''when '' then ''
```
