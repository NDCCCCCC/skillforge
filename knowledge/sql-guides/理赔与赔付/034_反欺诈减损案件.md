# 反欺诈减损案件


**涉及表/视图**: `ids_wuh.DWD_CAR_DD`, `ids_wuh.DWS_CASE_DD`, `ids_wuh.DWD_SPCL_INV_DD`, `ids_wuh.party_t`, `ids_wuh.DWD_CASE_DD`

## SQL / 正文

```sql
SELECT a.NOTIFICATIONNO 报案号  , a.CLAIMNOOFCOMMERCIAL 商业赔案号  , a.CLAIMNOOFCTP 交强赔案号  , a.COMMERCIALPOLICYNO 商业保单号  , a.CTPPOLICYNO 交强保单号  , a.INSURANTNAME 被保险人  , a.NOTIFYMANNAME 报案人  , a.NOTIFYTELEPHONE 报案电话  , (select string_agg(distinct DRIVERNAME,',') from ids_wuh.DWD_CAR_DD t where a.CASEFOLDERID = t.CASEFOLDERID) 双方驾驶员  , a.CASECREATETIME_F 商业险立案时间  , a.CASECREATETIME_M 交强险立案时间  , a.CASESETTLEDTIME_F 商业险案件结案时间  , a.CASESETTLEDTIME_M 交强险案件结案时间   , t.credential_no 被保人证件号  , a.PLATENO 承保车牌号  , e.ENGINENO 标的发动机号  , e.vin 标的车架号  , a.PLATENO1 出险时车牌号  , a.PLATENO0 三者车车牌号      , c.INVESTIGATIONREPORT 调查报告  , c.TOTALLOSSREDUCTIONAMOUNT 整案减损金额  , c.APPLICATIONREASON 申请原因         , c.REDUCELOSSREASON 减损原因  , c.INVESTIGATIONAUDITRESULT 调查项审核结论  , c.REFUSEREASON 调查项审核说明  , f.LASTENDCASETIME 整案最后结案时间FROM ids_wuh.DWS_CASE_DD a join (select t.*,(row_number() over (partition by CASEFOLDERID order by REPORTAUDITDATE desc)) rn from ids_wuh.DWD_SPCL_INV_DD t) con a.CASEFOLDERID = c.CASEFOLDERIDand c.rn = 1left join ids_wuh.party_t ton a.COMMERCIALPOLICYNO = t.policy_no and t.role = '2'join ids_wuh.DWD_CAR_DD eon a.CASEFOLDERID = e.CASEFOLDERIDand e.ISOBJECT = '1'join ids_wuh.DWD_CASE_DD fon a.CASEFOLDERID = f.CASEFOLDERIDwhere LASTENDCASETIME between TO_DATE('20241201 00:00:00','YYYYMMDD hh24:mi:ss') and TO_DATE('20250531 23:59:59','YYYYMMDD hh24:mi:ss')and TOTALLOSSREDUCTIONAMOUNT <> 0
```
