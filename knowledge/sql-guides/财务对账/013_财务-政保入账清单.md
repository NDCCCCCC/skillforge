# 财务-政保入账清单


**涉及表/视图**: `ETL_NONAUTO_POLICY_T`, `etl_payment_due_t`, `ids_wuh.TYPECOMP_T`, `ids_wuh.NONAUTO_CF_INDEMNITY_T`, `ids_wuh.r_rb_wjpk_t`, `ids_wuh.NONAUTO_CLAIM_FOLDER_T`, `ids_wuh.reinsurance_t`

**输出字段示例**: POLICY_NO, ENDORSEMENT_NO, DD, RZBF_NOTAX, SSBF_NOTAX, RZBF_WITH_TAX, SSBF_WITH_TAX, A.VAT_TAX+A.TRAVEL_TAX

## SQL / 正文

```sql
select b.POLICY_NO AS "POLICY_NO",b.ENDORSEMENT_NO AS "ENDORSEMENT_NO",b.DD AS "DD",b.RZBF_NOTAX AS "RZBF_NOTAX",b.ssbf_notax AS "SSBF_NOTAX",b.Rzbf_With_Tax AS "RZBF_WITH_TAX",b.Ssbf_With_Tax AS "SSBF_WITH_TAX",b.Vat_Tax+b.TRAVEL_TAX as "A.VAT_TAX+A.TRAVEL_TAX",b.DEPARTMENT_GROUP_CODE AS "DEPARTMENT_GROUP_CODE",b.DEPARTMENT_GROUP_NAME AS "DEPARTMENT_GROUP_NAME",b.DEPARTMENT_CODE AS "DEPARTMENT_CODE",b.TJBMMC AS "TJBMMC",a.TB_NAME AS "TB_NAME",a.INCEPTION_YEAR AS "INCEPTION_YEAR",a.ISSUE_DATE AS "ISSUE_DATE",a.INCEPTION_DATE AS "INCEPTION_DATE",a.PLANNED_END_DATE AS "PLANNED_END_DATE",a.PRODUCT_CODE AS "PRODUCT_CODE",a.product_name as "PRODUCT_NAME",a.kq  AS "KQ",a.kq_new AS "KQ_NEW",t.cwxzh "CWXZH",t.cwxzhmc "CWXZHMC",a.xzhdlmc AS "XZHDLMC",nvl(c.yjpf,0) as yjpf ,nvl(d.wjje,0) as wjje,e.reinsurance_percent_2 from ETL_NONAUTO_POLICY_T ajoin etl_payment_due_t b on a.policy_no = b.policy_no and b.dd<=to_char(to_date('2026-03-31'),'yyyymmdd')left join ids_wuh.TYPECOMP_T t on t.yxzh=b.product_codeleft join (select policy_no,SUM(insured_object_indemnity+investigation_fee+             investigation_agent_fee+inside_investigation_agent_fee+             claim_audit_fee+salvage_fee+other_fee-             salvage_recovery)as yjpf              from ids_wuh.NONAUTO_CF_INDEMNITY_T WHERE TRUNC(POSTED_DATE)<TRUNC(to_date('2026-03-31'))              group by policy_no) c on c.POLICY_no=a.POLICY_noleft join (select b.policy_no,sum(nvl(a.gsje,0)) wjje            from ids_wuh.r_rb_wjpk_t a left join ids_wuh.NONAUTO_CLAIM_FOLDER_T b             ON a.claim_folder_no = b.claim_folder_no            where a.flag_auto = '0' and trunc(a.list_date)=TRUNC(to_date('2026-03-31'))            group by b.policy_no) d on d.policy_no=a.POLICY_NOleft join ids_wuh.reinsurance_t e on e.reinsurance_policy_no=a.POLICY_NOwhere a.INCEPTION_DATE between to_date('20180101 00:00:00','yyyymmdd hh24:mi:ss')                       and trunc(to_date('2026-03-31'))-INTERVAL '1' secondand a.PRODUCT_CODE in (select yxzh from ids_wuh.TYPECOMP_T  where cwxzh in ('P150','P152'))and a.kq_new in ('政保客户','0','政保健康')and b.policy_no = 'AWUH07HI7325QAAAAA1Y'
```
