# v0.8 Milestone Completion — SkillForge Harness（可信×行动融合）

## Headline

v0.8 took SkillForge from a *trustworthy read-only RAG kernel* to a **harness**: memory, human-gated write tools, a planning layer, and sub-agent fan-out — all six phases (8–13) closed hermetically green, with every phase carrying its own `scripts/verify-phase-NN.sh` gate script. The milestone is the first closed under the **three-lock regime**: `compliance.lock.json` (Phase 8 pin, `5cf700f3…`), `hitl.lock.json` (Phase 11, `6e91881f…`), and `fanout.lock.json` (Phase 13, `af3020fd…`) — regeneration of any of them is defined as tampering (T-8-12), and three separate gate scripts re-verify their sha256 on every run.

Live evidence, not just hermetic evidence, backs the headline claims: Phase 8's frozen eval re-run against real GLM-5.2 + Milvus + BGE-M3 produced **faithfulness 0.9720 ≥ 0.9 gate** and **refusal_accuracy 95% (19/20)** across 50 cases; Phase 9's history-injection spike ran 35 live cases for a measured **$0.0481** and returned an honest, recorded **NO-GO** that reshaped Phase 10's design rather than being papered over; Phase 13's 20-case fan-out UAT measured **20/20 dispatched, 6/6 tier-mismatch rejections, 0.0 tier-closure regression** for **$2.92**.

The through-line invariant held everywhere: **citation whitelist = this turn's retrieval results, enforced in code** — history, summaries, sub-agent outputs, and resume-injected text can never become evidence. Phase 9 proved it adversarially (SC-3 malicious zero-penetration 0/10 PASS even while the gate failed on refusal drift), and Phase 13 proved it structurally (sub-agents inherit tier closure at construction; `subManifest.Writes = []`).

What remains operator-gated at write time: the **Phase 10 live 35-case `--via-pipeline` re-run** (GEVL-08/N9-V2 assigned this to the *milestone*, not to a phase — blocked on API reachability since 2026-08-18, re-attempted and rate-limited 2026-08-21), the **`git push origin master`** remote sync, the **`v0.8.0` annotated tag**, and the Phase 7 demo-GIF leftover. This report closes the DIST-09 half of the Phase 7 publication gate; the GIF half stays open. Every one of those is a row in the Operator Gates Ledger below, with evidence — never silently dropped.
