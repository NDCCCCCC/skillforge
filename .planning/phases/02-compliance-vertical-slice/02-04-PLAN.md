## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-2-30 | Tampering (V5 ASVS — malformed input) | validateSkillHandler JSON parse | mitigate | json.Unmarshal error → 400 with `{"valid":false,"error":"malformed json: ..."}`.
